Research Interests

I’m broadly interested in embodied AI. I’m working toward building agents that can use their bodies, virtual or physical, to interact with the world around them.

Research

World Models as Native Latent Action Models

Qinyue Li, Weijie Qiao, Qingyi Tao, Zerui Gong, Zhe Yang, Quanzhou Li, Taolin Zhang, Jinglin He, Zhonghua Wu, Chen Change Loy

arXiv, 2026

We extract compact latent actions from a pretrained video world model and use them to pretrain PAWlicy, a vision-language-action policy, on human, robot, and simulated videos.

Has GPT-6-Astra Achieved Embodied Intelligence?

Joint Research Team (Core Contributor)

Technical report, 2026

We evaluate GPT-6-Astra across 12 embodied benchmarks and eight real-robot platforms, examining how in-context learning, execution budgets, and the control harness affect performance.

DHAGrasp: Synthesizing Affordance-Aware Dual-Hand Grasps with Text Instructions

Quanzhou Li, Zhonghua Wu, Jingbo Wang, Chen Change Loy, Bo Dai

arXiv, 2025

We propose SymOpt for large-scale dual-hand grasp data generation and DHAGrasp, a text-guided two-stage model that synthesizes diverse dual-hand affordance-aware grasps for unseen objects.

Task-Oriented Human-Object Interactions Generation with Implicit Neural Representations

Quanzhou Li, Jingbo Wang, Chen Change Loy, Bo Dai

WACV 2024

We propose a method that generates full human-object interaction motions to conduct specific tasks. Our generated motions are continuous and allow upsampling to arbitrary frames.

Learning by Watching: Physical Imitation of Manipulation Skills from Human Videos

Haoyu Xiong, Quanzhou Li, Yun-Chun Chen, Homanga Bharadhwaj, Samrath Sinha, Animesh Garg

IROS 2021

We develop a perception module that learns to translate human videos to the robot domain followed by keypoint detection learned in an unsupervised fashion. The key insight of our method lies in explicitly exploiting the kinematics and motion information embedded in the video to learn structured representations.
